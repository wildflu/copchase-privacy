Privacy Policy for Cop Chaser

Last updated: 10/17/2016

we publishes the mobile game Cop Chaser. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email address or location. Your progress (coins, unlocked cars, best time and settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and some other regions, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice any time from the privacy settings link in the game, where it is shown, and you can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings. You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 7, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, uninstalling the game deletes it.

Changes

We may update this policy, and the date above will change when we do.
